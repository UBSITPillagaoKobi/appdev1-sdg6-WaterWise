# appdev1--sdg6-WaterWise

SDG 6 - CLEAN WATER AND SANITATION
Water quality monitor, sanitation access tracker, water conservation guide



Rationale:
Access to safe water, sanitaion and hygiene is a fundamental human need essential
for health and well-being. By focusing on SDG 6, our single-page application aims
spread awareness about water conservation, highlight local sanitation initiatives
and provide the community with accessible resources to improve local water management.
