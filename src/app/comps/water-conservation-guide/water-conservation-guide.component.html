Water Conservation Guide

Practical tips and strategies to conserve water in your daily life around Baguio City.

🚿 Shower Smarter

Take shorter showers (5-10 minutes) and install low-flow showerheads to reduce water usage by up to 50%.

🍽️ Kitchen Efficiency

Run full loads in dishwashers and washing machines. Fix leaky faucets immediately - they can waste 3,000 gallons per year.

🌱 Garden Wisely

Use drought-resistant plants, mulch to retain soil moisture, and water lawns/gardens during cooler parts of the day.

🚰 Faucet Habits

Turn off faucets while brushing teeth or soaping up. Collect rainwater for outdoor use when possible.

🌧️ Rainwater Harvesting

In Baguio, utilize the heavy rainy season by setting up catchment barrels. Use this water for flushing toilets and watering indoor plants.

🚽 Dual-Flush Retrofit

Replace old toilets with dual-flush models or place a weighted bottle in the tank to displace water and reduce the volume used per flush.

🧼 Eco-Friendly Laundry

Only run your washing machine with a full load. Use cold water cycles to save energy and choose high-efficiency detergents that require less rinsing.

🧺 Greywater Reuse

Collect the water used for washing vegetables or the "rinse" water from laundry (greywater) to clean your outdoor driveways or patio areas.

🥩 Defrosting Safely

Avoid running the tap to thaw frozen meat. Instead, plan ahead and thaw food in the refrigerator overnight to save gallons of running water.

🌲 Pine Needle Mulching

Use Baguio’s abundant pine needles as mulch in your garden. They help retain soil moisture and reduce the need for frequent watering.

🧹 Sweep, Don't Hose

Clean your sidewalk or storefront with a broom rather than a hose. Hosing down a driveway can waste over 100 gallons of water in just minutes.

🧊 Ice Cube Recycling

If you accidentally drop ice cubes, don't throw them in the sink. Place them in a potted plant instead to provide a slow-drip watering effect.

🐕 Pet Bathing Tips

Wash your pets in an area of the lawn that needs water. Use a bucket and sponge rather than a running hose to control water flow.

🕵️ Leak Detection

Check your water meter before and after a two-hour period when no water is being used. If the meter changes, you have a hidden leak.
