The Balili River Challenge

Unlike flat cities, Baguio's terrain requires advanced sanitation engineering. Currently, the city focus is on Zero Open Defecation (ZOD) status for all outlying barangays and the strict installation of grease traps for commercial establishments along Session Road and the Public Market to prevent blockages in the mountainous sewer network.

  • Tourist Impact: Sanitation services scale up by 300% during Panagbenga Season.
  • Waste Management: 100% of collected septage is treated before release into local creeks.
